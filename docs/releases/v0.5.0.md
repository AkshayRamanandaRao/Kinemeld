Kinemeld 0.5.0 lets you set up joints from the CAD itself.

### Highlights
- **Place from CAD feature.** Pick a bore, shaft, pin or planar face of an imported STEP part; both joint ends land on it, so anchors coincide and hinge axes agree by construction. Hover shows the feature first, e.g. *Cylinder Ø55.0 mm · axis −x*.
- **Drag to snap.** Drag a joint symbol in the 3D view onto a face and it snaps to that face's axis or plane.
- **Frames from CAD.** Put a named reference frame on a feature, with local +Z along its axis or normal; linked joints follow.
- **Measure in the view.** Distance, components and axis angle between any two picked features (M).

Picking works on cylinders and planes at the model's initial configuration. See `docs/workspaces.md` (Placing joints and frames from CAD) and `CHANGELOG.md`.
