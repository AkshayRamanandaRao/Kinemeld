Kinemeld 0.3.0 adds **rotary drives**: prescribe the angle of any revolute joint and MBDyn returns the torque the motor must deliver.

### Highlights
- **Motor and oscillation laws** for revolute joints, both starting smoothly from rest, edited in the joint inspector.
- **Inverse dynamics results**: measured and commanded angle, speed, motor torque, power and work for every driven joint — plotted, at full resolution in the CSV, and verified against rigid-body dynamics.
- **New featured study — Robot arm inverse dynamics**: a three-axis arm whose drive work matches its change in mechanical energy exactly.

See `docs/rotary-drives.md` and `CHANGELOG.md`. Downloads follow the same layout as 0.2: Linux and macOS archives bundle MBDyn 1.7.3; Windows uses an MBDyn configured through WSL.
