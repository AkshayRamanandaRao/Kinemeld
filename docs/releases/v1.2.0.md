Kinemeld 1.2.0: CAD part by part, shown end to end on an aircraft nose gear.

### Highlights
- **Add STEP files.** Once CAD is loaded, add more STEP files one part at a time. Earlier parts keep their attachments, every part is listed with its file, and Undo removes the last addition.
- **Pin holes from SolidWorks files.** Hole rims written as several arcs are now recognised, so joints can be placed by pointing at the pin holes of CAD from SolidWorks and similar tools.
- **New end-to-end showcase.** The homepage recording builds an aircraft nose gear from its eleven original STEP parts: imported one by one, joints placed on the CAD pin holes, mass from the solids, an 80 mm shock-strut stroke solved with MBDyn, and the torque-link fold and strut force in post-processing.
- **Name your model.** A new 3D assembly can be named in its properties.
- The README now plays the showcase recordings inline.

Projects from 1.0 and 1.1 open unchanged. See `CHANGELOG.md`.
