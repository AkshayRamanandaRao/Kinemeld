Kinemeld 1.1.0: contact friction.

### Highlights
- **Friction on contacts.** Sphere-plane contacts take a Coulomb friction coefficient. MBDyn applies the friction at the contact point, so sliding parts slow down, balls spin up and wheels drag their axles. Results show the solver's friction force, the slip speed and the energy friction absorbs.
- **New demo: landing-gear spin-up drag.** A wheel spun backwards to 30 m/s is dropped on a still platform, as drop-test rigs reproduce touchdown. Friction stops the spin about 50 ms after touchdown and loads the axle aft; the included study compares friction coefficients 0.3, 0.55 and 0.8.
- **Checked against physics.** A sliding ball ends up rolling at 5/7 of its speed, blocks stop at the predicted distance on tilted planes, and in the spin-up study momentum, Coulomb drag and energy all balance within 0.07 %.
