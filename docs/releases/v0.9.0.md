Kinemeld 0.9.0 makes building and checking a model in the 3D view faster.

### Highlights
- **Pick hole rims.** Circular edges — bolt holes, bores, shaft ends — are drawn while you pick and snap a joint, frame or measurement to their centre and axis.
- **Drag along the axis.** Drag a joint symbol to slide it along its own axis in 1 mm steps; Shift ignores the CAD under the pointer, Alt moves across the axis. Over a face or rim it still snaps to that feature.
- **Measure during the motion.** In Post-processing, Measure reads the frame on screen: distances and angles between features at the solved pose, with the time stated.

See `docs/workspaces.md` (Placing joints and frames from CAD) and `CHANGELOG.md`.
