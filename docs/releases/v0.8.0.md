Kinemeld 0.8.0 puts parameter studies into the demo set, each one checked case by case against independent physics.

### Highlights
- **Landing-gear sink speed.** The oleo-pneumatic gear drop test opens with a study of sink speeds from 1.5 to 3 m/s. The peak tyre load rises from 18.0 to 27.5 kN, and in every case the energy books balance within 0.1 % of the impact energy.
- **Wheel-end tyre stiffness.** Five contact stiffnesses from 10 to 40 kN/m; every peak tyre load matches an independent drop calculation within 0.5 %.
- **Studies travel with projects.** Featured studies open with their study preloaded and their response plotted; your own projects save and restore the study set-up.
- **Drop and sink speeds.** A new *All bodies* input sweeps an initial velocity shared by every body.

Watch the new recording on the homepage, or open **Examples → Featured studies → Oleo-pneumatic gear drop test**. See `docs/featured-studies.md` and `CHANGELOG.md`.
