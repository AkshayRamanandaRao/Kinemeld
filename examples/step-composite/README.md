# Two-part STEP mass example

These two original, generated STEP bricks and the starting spatial model accompany [the worked mass-property example](../../docs/step-mass.md#worked-example-two-step-parts-on-one-body). Each brick measures 0.1 × 0.2 × 0.3 m, is rotated 30° about world Z, and is centered at X = −0.15 m or +0.15 m. At 1000 kg/m³ the two solids total 12 kg. Their material density is an explicit example assumption, not metadata supplied by STEP.

Open `composite-body.mbdyn-studio`, attach both STEP parts to Bob, then calculate their combined properties as described in the guide. The supplied geometry and model are illustrative; they are not a validated engineering assembly.
