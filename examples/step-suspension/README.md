These are individual STEP exports, not an exported STEP assembly hierarchy with joint definitions. Their file names identify the parts; their placement and alignment must be checked and explicitly mapped to simulation body frames. Do not infer mass, inertia, constraints, CAD joints, or verified dynamics from visual geometry. They are demonstration CAD, not a production-validated suspension design. The MBDyn quarter-car model is a reduced vertical model, not this double-wishbone mechanism.
